# Advert features: flyers and social media

Saved for later. The app is **CarYard** (caryardng.com): sellers list cars, buyers buy, lenders offer loans.
Before claiming "only us" in paid adverts, check what Jiji, Cars45, Autochek and others offer at the time; "built in" and
"free" are safer words.

## What sets us apart

### For buyers

1. **Find, finance and buy in one place**: search, compare, book an inspection, make an offer, trade in and apply for a car loan.
2. **Many kinds of lenders, not just banks**: commercial banks, microfinance banks, finance companies, licensed money lenders and
   individual lenders. Buyers only see lenders whose rates, amounts and states fit the car.
3. **We never touch your money**: buyers pay the seller directly. No middleman account, no "pay to unlock" fees.
4. **Loan details stay private**: income, employer and documents go only to the lender the buyer picks, never to the seller.
5. **Filters that matter in Nigeria**: customs duty paid, registered in Nigeria, inspected, verified seller, car loans available,
   takes trade-ins, open to offers.
6. **Know what you can afford first**: a budget calculator that tags every car within reach.
7. **Live "I'm on my way" location** shared with the seller for that visit only, never stored.
8. **Price-drop alerts on WhatsApp** for saved searches and saved cars.

### For sellers

1. **Your own website, free**: a mini-site with its own link (your own domain on Enterprise).
2. **WhatsApp share cards**: ready-made price images for Status and Instagram, with tracked links that show which shares brought buyers.
3. **QR posters and windscreen stickers** for every car: passers-by scan and see photos, price and a monthly estimate.
4. **Sales Manager for walk-ins**: cash, transfer and POS, part-payments, instalment plans and papers. Works offline.
5. **Profit per car**: what each car cost and what it made.
6. **Buyers arrive with loans in progress**: the seller hears when a buyer is approved, without seeing private details.
7. **Auto-post to Facebook and Instagram** when a car is published.

### For lenders

1. **Applications come with the car**: price, deposit and loan length.
2. **Buyers only see you when the car fits** your rates, amounts and states.
3. **Portal or your own system (API)**; you decide and close the loan your way.
