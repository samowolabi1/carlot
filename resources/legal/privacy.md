## 1. The data we collect

**You give us:**

- Account details: name, phone number and/or email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash), and whether you signed in with Google.
- Enquiries and activity: chats with Lots, bookings, offers, reservations, trade-in details and photos, reviews, reports and support tickets.
- **Car loan applications** (only if you apply): your name, phone, email, monthly income, monthly loan and rent commitments, type of work and employer, the car, deposit and loan length, and documents you choose to upload (for example bank statements or ID).
- For Lots: business name, CAC number and certificate, address and map pin, bank account for buyers' payments, staff accounts, stock, sales, payments and receipts recorded in Lot Manager, and buyer details entered by the Lot.
- For lenders: company or, for individual lenders, personal identity details (name, NIN and ID), licence details and copy, contact details, team members and loan products.

**We collect automatically:** device and browser type, IP address, pages and cars viewed, searches, share-link clicks and similar usage information, and security logs. With your permission, your device's location is used in your browser to sort cars by distance; **we do not store your location**. If you share live location for a visit, only your latest position is kept, and only until the visit ends.

**From others:** Lots may add you to their customer book when you enquire or buy; lenders send their decisions and messages about your application; company-registry services (for example Dojah) return the registered details of a Lot's CAC number; Google gives us your name and email if you sign in with Google.

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

## 3. Who we share it with

- **The Lot you contact** receives your name, and your phone number once you engage with them (it is masked before then), and the details of your enquiry, booking, offer, reservation or trade-in. Lots are controllers of the data they hold in their customer book.
- **The lender you choose** receives your car loan application and documents. The lender is a controller in its own right and will process your data under its own privacy notice and banking laws (including identity and credit checks with licensed credit bureaus under the Credit Reporting Act 2017, which happen at the lender, not on LotLink). **The Lot never sees your income, commitments, employer, messages with the lender, documents or a decline**; it is only told that you applied, and, if it happens, that you were pre-approved or approved (with the amount) and that the lender paid it.
- **Service providers (processors)** who act on our instructions under contracts that protect your data: WhatsApp (Meta) and SMS providers (for example Termii) for messages; email delivery; Paystack and Flutterwave for Lots' payments to LotLink; Cloudflare and our hosting providers for storage and delivery; Meilisearch for search; Google (sign-in and maps); Dojah for company-registry checks.
- **Authorities** when the law requires it, or to protect people's safety or prevent fraud.
- A buyer of our business, under the same protections.

We do not sell your personal data.

## 5. How long we keep it

- Account data: while your account is open. If you close your account it is hidden at once, you can restore it by signing in within **30 days**, and after that your personal details are removed or anonymised.
- Car loan applications: the details you shared and any documents are deleted **24 months** after the application is closed (declined, withdrawn, couldn't send, or paid to the lot), keeping only a record that it existed and its outcome.
- Chats, bookings, offers and reviews: while your account is open (the Lot keeps its own copy of your enquiry in its customer book).
- Billing and tax records: 6 years. Audit and security logs: as long as needed for security and legal claims, normally no more than 6 years.
- Lot Manager sales records are kept for the Lot while its account is open (the Lot is responsible for its own records).

## 6. How we protect it

Encryption in transit (HTTPS/HSTS) and, for sensitive fields such as loan applications and lenders' API keys, encryption at rest; private storage behind short-lived signed links for documents, CAC certificates and licences; one-time sign-in codes with limits; two-factor authentication for LotLink staff; role-based access for Lot and lender teams; audit logs of changes to money, status and staff "log in as" support sessions; and regular security reviews. If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within **72 hours** and tell you without undue delay where required.

## 7. Your rights (NDPA sections 34 to 38)

You have the right to: be informed; **access** your data and get a copy; **correct** it; **delete** it; **restrict** or **object** to processing (including direct marketing, which you can stop at any time from Account → Notifications or the unsubscribe link); **data portability**; **withdraw consent** at any time (for example to future car loan processing); and not to be subject to solely automated decisions with significant effects.

You can close your account and delete your data yourself from Account → Privacy and my data. For anything else email {privacy_email}. We will reply within **30 days**, free of charge unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive. We may ask you to prove your identity.

If you are not happy with our answer you can complain to the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission** (ndpc.gov.ng).
